## 🧠 Abstract

When we talk about how AI models (or human minds) resolve meaning, we're often unknowingly describing a kind of **asynchronous inference logic**. Just like JavaScript's `Promise.then()` chains and `async/await` syntax handle uncertain outcomes across time, so too does a cognitive model resolve ambiguity, predict outcomes, and deduce relevance. This article introduces the **core mechanics of Promises and async functions** to explain how `<undefined>` meaning becomes defined in a rapidly resolving interpretive state.
